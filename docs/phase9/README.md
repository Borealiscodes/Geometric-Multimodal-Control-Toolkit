# Phase 9 — Seed Geometry Activation
## Geometric-Multimodal-Control-Toolkit (GMC Toolkit)

Phase 9 activates the first geometry-bearing layer of the GMC Toolkit: the
projection-seed geometry system. Projection-seeds unify holonomy, ecology
curvature, ecology alignment, world-rule collapse proximity, and geometry
operators into a single G-Layer structure.

Phase 9 introduces six batches:

1. **Batch 15 — Projection-Seed Generator**  
   Generates seeds from holonomy, ecology curvature, ecology alignment, and
   world-rule collapse proximity.

2. **Batch 16 — Seed Curvature Operator**  
   Applies curvature transforms using ecology curvature, alignment modulation,
   and holonomy-curvature coupling.

3. **Batch 17 — Seed Alignment Operator**  
   Applies alignment transforms using ecology alignment and holonomy-alignment
   coupling.

4. **Batch 18 — Seed Holonomy Response Operator**  
   Applies holonomy-driven curvature, alignment, and gradient modulation.

5. **Batch 19 — Seed Collapse Coupler**  
   Applies collapse-surface proximity transforms and pre-Phase-10 collapse
   coupling.

6. **Batch 20 — Seed Geometry Envelope**  
   Unifies curvature, alignment, holonomy response, and collapse proximity into
   a stable geometry envelope.

---

## Purpose of Phase 9

Phase 9 establishes the geometry foundation required for Phase 10 collapse-surface
activation. Projection-seeds serve as the geometry primitives that unify holonomy,
ecology, and world-rule signals. The Seed Geometry Envelope provides a stable
G-Layer structure that Phase 10 will use to activate collapse-surface geometry.

Phase 9 does not activate collapse geometry; it prepares the envelope for it.
