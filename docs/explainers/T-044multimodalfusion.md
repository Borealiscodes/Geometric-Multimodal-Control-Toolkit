🌿 Accessible Tile — What Is Multimodal Fusion?

Imagine three friends describing the same object 🌿.

One draws it.  
One describes it.  
One makes a sound that reminds them of it.

To understand the object, you combine:

- the picture,  
- the words,  
- the sound.

Your brain fuses them into one meaning.

AI does the same thing.

Multimodal fusion is how models learn:

- what a picture means,  
- what a sound represents,  
- what a sentence refers to,  
- and how they all connect.

Fusion is the shared meaning space.

🔧 Engineering Tile — Why Fusion Matters

Fusion determines whether modalities:

- align

Fusion is the glue of multimodal intelligence.
