🐐 Accessible Tile — What Is Curvature?

Imagine a goat 🐐 walking across hills.

If the ground is flat → the goat walks straight.  
If the ground curves → the goat’s path bends.  
If the ground dips → the goat speeds up.  
If the ground rises → the goat slows down.  

Curvature is the shape of the landscape.

AI has landscapes too — inside its latent space.

Curvature tells the model:

- where meaning bends  
- where concepts cluster  
- where ideas collapse  
- where transitions become unstable  

Curvature is the “hilliness” of thought.

📐 Geometrist Tile — The Math Behind the Hills

Curvature is encoded in the Riemann curvature tensor:

\[
R{ijkl} = \partialk \Gamma{ijl} - \partiall \Gamma_{ijk}
           + \Gamma{imk}\Gamma{mjl} - \Gamma{iml}\Gamma{mjk}
\]

Scalar curvature:

\[
\kappa(z) = g^{ij} R_{ij}
\]

Where:

- \( g^{ij} \) is the metric  
- \( R_{ij} \) is the Ricci curvature  
- \( \Gamma_{ijk} \) are Christoffel symbols  

Interpretation:

- \( \kappa(z) > 0 \) → sphere‑like, contracting  
- \( \kappa(z) = 0 \) → flat, stable  
- \( \kappa(z) < 0 \) → saddle‑like, collapsing  

Curvature is the second derivative of meaning.
